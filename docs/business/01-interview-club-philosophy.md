# B01 — Club DNA, identity and managerial philosophy

> **Fictional interview.** No quoted answer here is a genuine statement by Sir Alex Ferguson.

### Q1. What must a manager decide before choosing 4-3-3 or 4-4-2?
**Imagined Ferguson answer:** "A shape is a tool. The club needs principles that remain recognizable when the lineup and opponent change."

**Business interpretation:** Define game model separately from formation: how to advance the ball, how much defensive risk is acceptable, preferred ball-recovery location, transition priorities, acceptable squad age profile, and youth pathway. The same identity may have several formation implementations.

**Manager Hub rules:**
- Store Club DNA as explicit constraints + weighted preferences, not an AI-generated adjective alone.
- Record rationale, author, review date and confidence. Allow a manager to override recommendations.
- A tactic is acceptable when it meets minimum safety constraints; maximizing stylistic similarity is secondary to feasibility with available personnel.

### Q2. Should the manager change philosophy when players are unsuitable?
**Imagined answer:** "Keep your direction; adapt the route." 
- Distinguish **non-negotiable** (effort, defensive responsibility, budget discipline) from **adaptable** (build-up route, pressing height, width).
- Build two plans: **current squad best-fit** and **target identity after recruitment**. Expose position-by-position gaps between them.
- Test a style change against personnel, training time, tactical familiarity and schedule; do not present instant adaptation as guaranteed.

### Q3. How should board objectives influence the football model?
**Imagined answer:** "A club must know what success costs, and what it can afford."
- Align results, playing style, youth minutes, net spend and wages across competing board objectives.
- Create a priority hierarchy; display tradeoffs (e.g., top-four target vs selling the starting striker).
- Set time horizon: 0–12 months, 1–3 years, 3–5 years.

### Q4. How do we know a philosophy is working?
- Outcome: points, goal difference, qualification and trophies, adjusted for context.
- Process: chance creation/concession, possession progression, box entries, rest-defence exposure, pressing effectiveness when available.
- Development: minutes by age group, academy progression, succession readiness.
- Avoid attributing a result to philosophy from 1–2 matches; note opponent quality and sample size.

### Example product scenario
An elite pressing identity is selected, but the current CB pair has limited pace and the forward line cannot sustain intense pressure. Recommend a less exposed line or different pressing trigger **for the interim**, flag recruitment priorities and explain the cost of forcing the target setup.

**Outputs:** Club DNA profile; current-vs-target fit; tactical guardrails; seasonal goal tree.
