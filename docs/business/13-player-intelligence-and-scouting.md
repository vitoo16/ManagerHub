# B13 — Player Intelligence & Scouting

Feature proposal: estimated CA from available attributes; position and role suitability; football charts and contextual transfer advice.

Definitions: true CA (verified FM field), estimated CA (our model), RCA-style attribute budget estimate, Role Fit, and signing recommendation are separate outputs. A score must name its source and uncertainty. No claim to reproduce the proprietary FM ability formula.

Read-only first. Do not infer PA from attributes alone. If player minutes or event data are missing, hide performance charts rather than generate statistics.
