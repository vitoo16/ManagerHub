# B06 — Academy, youth, player development and retraining

> Fictional, illustrative answers only.

### Q1. How do you build a lasting club?
**Imagined answer:** "You must develop tomorrow's first team while competing today."
Build a pipeline by age group and football function, not just sorted PA. Evaluate opportunity to play, coaching/training context, age, role, physical readiness, mentality and succession opportunities.

### Q2. How should a player change positions?
Assess transferable strengths, weaknesses, preferred foot, defensive demands, familiarity and timeline. A striker converted to right-back must be evaluated for positioning, tackling, defensive reactions, recovery runs and opponent matchup, not just stamina.

### Q3. Should a wonderkid start immediately?
**Imagined answer:** "Only if playing him helps him and the team."
Recommend clear pathways: first-team rotation, domestic cups, affiliate loan, mentoring, targeted retraining. Model expected minutes, exposure quality and blocking senior players. Do not imply guaranteed development.

### Q4. How is development measured?
- Attribute change over save snapshots with stable player identity and consistent scale.
- CA delta and PA headroom if verified and allowed in Editor Mode.
- Competitive minutes, role-specific actions, injuries and adaptation.
- Quality of evidence: changes may be from database patches, edited values or incomplete snapshots.

### Q5. What is succession readiness?
A combination of minimum role competence, minutes, tactical fit, durability and confidence. PA alone does not qualify. Keep a human override and clear explanation.
