# B09 — Leadership, dressing room and human context

> Imagined interview only; no attribution of these words to Ferguson.

### Q1. Why does a leader matter beyond ability?
**Imagined answer:** "The standard is set every day, not only in matches."
Differentiate ability, leadership, professionalism, influence, seniority and fit with squad culture; avoid deriving a person's moral character from hidden numeric values.

### Q2. Who should become captain?
Define leadership criteria (communication, reliability, status in group, availability and performance context), evidence and uncertainty. Show multiple candidates with tradeoffs; no black-box universal ranking.

### Q3. Should an unhappy star always be sold?
Examine reason, duration, contract, importance, replacement, morale effect and whether management action can address the issue. Prefer decision tree to absolute verdict.

### Q4. How to manage generational turnover?
The sporting case and dressing-room case may diverge. Veteran departures can free wages yet remove informal mentoring; record that as qualitative tradeoff unless game exposes relevant values.

### Q5. Can Manager Hub simulate human reactions?
Yes, as an **optional scenario model**, never as purported FM engine truth. Expose assumptions, editable parameters and no deterministic behavioral promises. Avoid labeling or stigmatizing real people.

**Outputs:** leadership map, squad hierarchy, conflict/contract tracker, change-impact notes, communication calendar.
