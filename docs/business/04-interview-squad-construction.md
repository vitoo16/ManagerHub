# B04 — Squad construction, balance, rotation and succession

> Fictional interview inspired by long-term club-building, not authentic quotations.

### Q1. How many excellent strikers should a team buy?
**Imagined answer:** "Buy what the system needs; then look at minutes and motivation."
Plan for **positions and functions**, not just raw headcount. Distinguish ST finishing, aerial threat, pressing, linking play and running channels. Two strikers may be excellent yet cover the same need.

### Q2. What is a complete squad?
- Best XI under current tactical constraints.
- Rotation XI that preserves key functionality.
- Emergency coverage when 2–3 players are unavailable.
- Youth pathway: realistic competitive minutes, not just "high PA" labels.
- Wage and registration constraints: homegrown rules, foreign limits, age and squad slots, version/league dependent.
- Functional redundancy: not every position needs two identical players; versatile players may cover multiple roles but cannot occupy two slots simultaneously.

### Q3. Should a reserve play out of position?
**Imagined answer:** "Tell me what breaks elsewhere when you move him."
The optimizer must recompute the **entire XI** after reallocating a utility player. For example, moving Endrick to full-back may increase right-side energy while weakening attack and removing another rotation option. Model opportunity cost and role-specific defensive ability, not just stamina/work rate.

### Q4. How do you plan succession?
For each critical role, record incumbent, internal successors, readiness threshold, forecast season, development plan, alternative recruit and contract deadlines. Timeline views: this season / 1–3 years / 3–5 years.

### Q5. When is a transfer necessary?
A transfer need is validated by **functional gap × impact × scarcity × urgency**, reduced by youth potential and tactical alternatives. Prefer scenario comparisons:
- Keep current team.
- Change system.
- Develop internal replacement.
- Recruit affordable starter.
- Sell expensive veteran and recruit two complementary players.

**Outputs:** depth chart, coverage matrix, best XI scenarios, succession planner, minutes allocation and risk alerts.
