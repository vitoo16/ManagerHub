# B11 — Building a dynasty, rebuild timing and club continuity

> This is a fictional interview inspired by Ferguson's documented focus on club foundation and youth.

### Q1. What survives a title-winning season?
**Imagined answer:** "The system that builds the next champion."
Monitor sustainability: key players aging together, expiring contracts, academy pipeline, quality of reserves, succession risk, wage concentration and tactical dependence.

### Q2. When is a rebuild necessary?
Use multi-dimensional triggers rather than a fixed age: declining minutes contribution, physical risk, lineup concentration, too few feasible replacements, contract cliff, financial strain. A single bad season is not proof.

### Q3. How many planning scenarios?
At least **contend now**, **balanced transition** and **aggressive rebuild**. Each scenario tracks competitive strength, squad age, development minutes, transfer spend, wages and number of unfilled tactical functions. Use confidence bands where modeled.

### Q4. What is the manager's legacy in an app?
Continuity, title cycles, academy promotion, tactical evolution, net squad value and resilience after key departures. Avoid pretending these are objective real-world leadership quality measurements.

### Q5. How do we revise a plan?
Take periodic save snapshots and compare actual outcomes with the plan. Provide an immutable decision history: what we believed, what we did, what changed, what evidence updated the view.

**Outputs:** Dynasty Planner (1/3/5 seasons), successor map, rebuild trigger board, snapshots and decision journal.
